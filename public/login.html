Sign in using your Google / Gmail account. Your verified identity creates your persistent citizen profile.

Why Google Popups Require Setup:

Google Identity Services enforces strict origin verification and requires a registered Google Cloud Client ID. Without it, Google blocks popup dialogs.

  1. Open Google Cloud Console.
  2. Create an OAuth 2.0 Client ID (Web Application).
  3. Add Authorized JavaScript Origins:
    http://localhost:3000
    https://your-app.vercel.app
  4. Add GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID=your_id_here to your .env file or Vercel Environment Variables.
