How Life on Aethel Works

A persistent text-based civilization combining life simulation, economics, and politics.

Natural Aging & Life

Start at Age 1. Growth is rapid from Age 1 to 5, transitioning smoothly into elementary school, teenage years, university, and adult careers.

5 Sovereign Nations

Live in Eldoria (Monarchy), Solaria (Democracy), Cyrene (Technocracy), Valona (Social Democracy), or Zephyria (Sultanate) with unique constitutions.

Virtual Economy

Work career shifts, collect salaries, manage personal savings, start businesses, trade items, and participate in national tax policies.

AI Citizens & Pets

Adopt and care for an AI pet companion (Dog, Cat, Owl, Rabbit), talk with local AI inhabitants, and read the daily newspaper chronicle.

Sovereign Civilizations of Aethel

Every nation offers distinct governance, legal rights, and economic frameworks.
